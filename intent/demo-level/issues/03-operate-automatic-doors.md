# 03: Operate automatic doors

**Source story:** 4

**What to build:** As a player, I want automatic doors to respond to both me and policemen, so that doors consistently shape movement, projectiles, and sight lines.

**Blocked by:** 01: Navigate the prison and alternate passage.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A closed door opens when approached by either the player or a policeman and remains open for the rest of the attempt.
- [ ] Closed doors block movement, sight, and projectiles; open doors do not.
- [ ] Door behavior works in the prison layout and is verified for both player and police activation.
- [ ] Opening a door exposes an observable hook for the required visual and audio feedback.
