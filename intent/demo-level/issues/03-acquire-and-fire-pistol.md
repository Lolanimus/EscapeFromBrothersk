# 03: Acquire and fire the pistol

**Source story:** 3

**What to build:** As a player, I want to acquire and fire a pistol, so that I have a precise baseline ranged weapon.

**Blocked by:** 01: Navigate the prison and alternate passage.

**Status:** done

- [x] The player can use E to acquire a pistol and left click to fire one accurate, fast projectile toward the mouse.
- [x] A player projectile permanently kills a living or knocked-down policeman in one hit.
- [x] Projectiles stop at walls, do not penetrate or ricochet, and have no headshot or damage-falloff behavior.
- [x] Pistol projectile speed and player firing cadence are selected as configurable values and verified through observable gameplay tests.
