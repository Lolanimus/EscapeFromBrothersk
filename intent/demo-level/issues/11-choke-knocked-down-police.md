# 11: Choke a knocked-down policeman

**Source story:** 11

**What to build:** As a player, I want to choke a knocked-down policeman through an interruptible hold action, so that I can permanently eliminate him without ammunition while accepting close-range risk.

**Blocked by:** 06: Apply the motivation combat loop; 10: Punch and recover knocked-down police.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Holding left click beside a knocked-down policeman for three uninterrupted seconds permanently kills him.
- [ ] The player cannot move while choking, and visible progress communicates the hold duration.
- [ ] Releasing left click, attempting to move, or taking damage cancels the choke and resets its progress.
- [ ] A completed choke produces the same permanent-death and motivation-restoration outcomes as other permanent kills.
