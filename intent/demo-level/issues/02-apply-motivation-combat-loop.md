# 02: Apply the motivation combat loop

**Source story:** 2

**What to build:** As a player, I want police attacks to reduce my motivation and permanent kills to restore it, so that survival and the elimination objective reinforce the same combat loop.

**Blocked by:** 03: Acquire and fire the pistol; 06: Pursue and shoot the detected player.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The player starts with 100 motivation, loses 25 motivation from each police bullet damage event, and dies at zero.
- [ ] Permanently killing a policeman restores 10 motivation up to a maximum of 100.
- [ ] Lower motivation introduces no additional mechanical or presentation penalty beyond proximity to death.
- [ ] Motivation changes, the cap, permanent-kill restoration, and death are verified through observable gameplay tests.
