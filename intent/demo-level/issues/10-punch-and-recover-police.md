# 10: Punch and recover knocked-down police

**Source story:** 10

**What to build:** As an unarmed player, I want to punch policemen unconscious and see when they are recovering, so that fists create a temporary opening rather than an immediate kill.

**Blocked by:** 01: Navigate the prison and alternate passage.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Left click while unarmed punches the targeted policeman and knocks him down without permanently killing him.
- [ ] A knocked-down policeman remains unconscious for 15 seconds and displays a visible warning during the final 3 seconds.
- [ ] A policeman who is not permanently killed recovers after 15 seconds and resumes the appropriate living state.
- [ ] Living, knocked-down, recovering, and dead police are observably distinct, and a knocked-down policeman can still be permanently killed by a projectile.
