# 15: Drop police weapons with full magazines

**Source story:** 15

**What to build:** As a player, I want permanently killed policemen to drop their guns with full magazines, so that combat can replenish my limited ranged ammunition.

**Blocked by:** 09: Carry and switch the limited loadout; 13: Acquire and fire the shotgun; 14: Acquire and fire the AK-47.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Every permanently killed policeman drops his equipped pistol, shotgun, or AK-47 with one full compatible magazine.
- [ ] A dropped weapon can be acquired or swapped through the standard interaction while preserving its specified ammunition state.
- [ ] Interacting with the same ranged weapon already carried collects only its full compatible magazine when capacity permits.
- [ ] Drops do not count toward the guaranteed placed-ammunition supply, and all weapon types are covered by observable loot tests.
